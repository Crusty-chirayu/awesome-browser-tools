# 🔒 Privacy & Security

[← Back to main list](../README.md)

### [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Safari  
Open-source password manager with a genuinely free tier and no dark patterns.

### [ClearURLs](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Strips tracking parameters (utm_, fbclid, etc.) from URLs automatically.

### [Decentraleyes](https://decentraleyes.org/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Serves common CDN-hosted libraries locally to stop third-party tracking requests.

### [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
EFF's tracker blocker that learns which trackers to block based on behavior, not lists.

### [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave  
Efficient wide-spectrum ad and content blocker. Fully open source, no "acceptable ads" program.

### [Avast Online Security & Privacy](https://www.avast.com/online-security-privacy)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Site safety ratings, tracker blocking, and phishing protection while you browse.

### [Cookie AutoDelete](https://github.com/Cookie-AutoDelete/Cookie-AutoDelete)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Automatically deletes cookies from closed tabs unless the domain is whitelisted.

### [DuckDuckGo Privacy Essentials](https://duckduckgo.com/app)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari  
Tracker blocking, forced encryption, and a privacy grade rating for every site.

### [Firefox Multi-Account Containers](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/multi-account-containers/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Firefox  
Isolates cookies/logins into separate colored tab containers (work, personal, shopping).

### [Ghostery](https://www.ghostery.com/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Blocks ads and trackers while showing exactly who's tracking you on each site.

### [KeePassXC-Browser](https://keepassxc.org/docs/keepassxc-browser-migration)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Connects your local KeePassXC password vault to autofill logins in-browser, fully offline-first.

### [Malwarebytes Browser Guard](https://www.malwarebytes.com/browserguard)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Blocks malware-hosting sites, scareware pop-ups, and tech-support scam pages.

### [NoScript](https://noscript.net/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Firefox, Chrome  
Whitelists which sites can run JavaScript/Flash, drastically reducing attack surface.

### [ProtonVPN](https://protonvpn.com/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Free-tier VPN extension from the makers of Proton Mail, no logs, unlimited data.

### [uMatrix](https://github.com/gorhill/uMatrix)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Firefox  
Advanced matrix-based blocker for scripts, frames, and requests per domain (power users).

### [Windscribe](https://windscribe.com/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Free VPN + ad/tracker blocker extension with a generous monthly data allowance.

### [Bitwarden Send](https://bitwarden.com/products/send/)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Securely share sensitive text/files with end-to-end encryption and expiry timers.

### [HTTPS Everywhere Successor: Smart HTTPS](https://github.com/AlanQuatermain/smart-https)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Firefox  
Forces sites to load over HTTPS whenever possible, reducing man-in-the-middle risk.

### [uBlock Origin Lite](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ublock-origin-lite)
**Category:** Privacy & Security · **Platforms:** Chrome  
Manifest V3-compliant lightweight ad/tracker blocker built for the new Chrome extension standard.
