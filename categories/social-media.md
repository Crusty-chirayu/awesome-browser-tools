# 📱 Social Media

[← Back to main list](../README.md)

### [News Feed Eradicator](https://www.newsfeederadicator.com/)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Replaces your social feeds with an inspirational quote to curb doomscrolling.

### [Unhook](https://unhook.app/)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Removes YouTube's recommended feed, comments, and other distracting elements.

### [SocialFocus](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/socialfocus)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome  
Blocks distracting parts of social sites while keeping core functionality (e.g. messaging).

### [Feedbro](https://nodetics.com/feedbro/)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Full-featured open-source RSS/Atom feed reader built into your browser.

### [Nimbus Note Web Clipper](https://nimbusweb.me/)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Clip posts, threads, and pages from social sites to organize and revisit later.

### [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome  
Shows real-time follower/subscriber stats and growth rank on YouTube, Twitch, and more.

### [Twitter Media Downloader](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/twitter-media-downloader)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Adds a download button to images, GIFs, and videos on X/Twitter.

### [SocialFocus Alt: Unfollow Everything](https://github.com/amika-sq/UnfollowEverything)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox  
Open-source tool to mass-unfollow on social platforms in bulk.

### [Tab Session Manager](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tab-session-manager)
**Category:** Social Media · **Platforms:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
Save and restore browsing sessions across devices, useful for social research/monitoring.
